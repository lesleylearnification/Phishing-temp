NOW SEE THE TELLS.

Across three different attacks, the same defensive habit matters: inspect what the message is asking you to trust before you act.

PASSWORDCheck the real sender, unexpected sign-in requests, urgency, destination cues, and whether the message fits your normal account workflow.
MONEYQuestion identity, unusual payment changes, pressure, deviations from normal business process, and requests that discourage independent verification.
MACHINECheck who sent the file, whether you expected it, the document premise, attachment or sharing cues, and pressure or curiosity designed to make you open it.
WHAT TO CHECK EVERY TIMESender. Context. Requested action. Destination or file. Pressure. If anything does not fit, verify through a trusted channel before acting.
